## 🎯 What Is PersoDub?

PersoDub is a free application that lets you **dub videos into another language while keeping the speaker's original voice**. It works entirely on your Mac — no internet connection needed, no cloud servers, no waiting for uploads. Your videos stay private on your computer.

The magic? PersoDub uses advanced speech recognition and voice cloning technology to translate what someone says, then recreates their voice speaking the translated words. The result sounds natural and matches the original speaker's tone and style — without stretching or distorting the audio timing.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use PersoDub on an Intel Mac?
PersoDub is optimized for Apple Silicon. While it may run slower on Intel Macs, it's not officially supported. For the best experience, we recommend using an Apple Silicon Mac.

### Do I need an internet connection?
No. PersoDub runs entirely offline. All processing happens locally on your Mac. The only time you'd need internet is to download the application itself.

### How accurate is the voice cloning?
PersoDub uses state-of-the-art speech-to-text and voice synthesis models. For clear, single-speaker videos, the results are remarkably accurate. Background noise or multiple speakers can reduce quality.

### Can I dub videos with multiple speakers?
PersoDub works best with videos featuring one main speaker. If multiple people talk, the app will focus on the loudest or most dominant voice.

### Will the video quality change?
No. PersoDub only replaces the audio track. The original video resolution and quality remain unchanged.

### How long can my videos be?
There's no strict limit, but longer videos require more time and disk space. A 1-hour video might take 30–60 minutes to process and need 10–15 GB of space.

### Is PersoDub free?
Yes, PersoDub is completely free and open-source. You can download, use, and modify it as you wish.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### PersoDub won't open
Make sure you're running macOS 13 or newer. Also, check that your Mac has an Apple Silicon chip (go to  → About This Mac → Chip).

### Processing is very slow
Close other heavy applications while PersoDub runs. Also, try shorter videos or lower-resolution exports if you're on an 8 GB RAM machine.

### The dubbed audio sounds robotic
This can happen with low-quality source audio. Try using a video with clearer speech and less background noise. You can also try reducing the video's original volume before processing.

### I get a "Not enough disk space" error
PersoDub needs temporary space during processing. Free up at least 5 GB and try again.
